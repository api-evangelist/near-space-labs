# Near Space Labs

> Near Space Labs delivers high-frequency, 7cm resolution aerial imagery at scale through a proprietary stratospheric data capture platform. Earth intelligence, without compromise.

Near Space Labs operates autonomous stratospheric vehicles at 65,000 feet to capture orthoimagery across 80% of the US, refreshed twice a year. Our imagery serves P&C insurance underwriting, state and local government GIS programs, utility and infrastructure monitoring, forestry and environmental monitoring, mapping technology platforms, and roofing and home services.
